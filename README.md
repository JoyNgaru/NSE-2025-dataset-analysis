# NSE-2025-dataset-analysis
After analysing the NSE 2025 dataset, I created an NSE Investment Guide 

# Six sections, all beginner-friendly:

## Top Performers 
A full table of the 9 best-performing companies with their growth, price movement, and risk level. Plus detailed cards for the Top 5 maximum-return picks with plain explanations of why each one grew.
## Safe Beginner Picks 
The 6 low-volatility companies broken into "Ultra-Safe" and "Good Growth + Low Risk" categories, each with a plain-English description of the business model.
## Best Months to Buy 
Visual bar charts showing the optimal entry months for EQTY, COOP, and EABL, with the reasoning behind each pattern (e.g. why December is cheapest for Equity).
## How to Buy Shares 
A 6-step process from zero to owning your first stock, including exactly what documents you need, what fees to expect, and how T+3 settlement works.
## Portfolio Strategies 
Three ready-made portfolios (Aggressive / Balanced / Conservative) shown as visual allocation bars, with expected returns and risk levels.
Glossary — 12 key terms explained in plain English (no jargon) — from "What is a dividend?" to "What is a CDS account?"

Every section includes a highlighted explainer box that answers the "but what does that actually mean?" question a first-time investor would ask.
